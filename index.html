Flagship Project

Jontro Soinik 1.0

EOD Remotely Operated Vehicle · Bangladesh Army · UN Peacekeeping

An EOD (Explosive Ordnance Disposal) Remotely Operated Vehicle designed, prototyped, manufactured, assembled, validated, and delivered entirely in Bangladesh. Systems of this class are produced by a handful of countries globally and typically cost hundreds of thousands of dollars per unit from international suppliers. This one was built in Dhaka.

Three units were delivered to DGDP against military acceptance criteria and deployed with the Bangladesh Army Bomb Disposal Unit for UN Peacekeeping Missions in Mali and the Democratic Republic of Congo. On 05 May 2024, the Bangladesh Army formally transferred the system to the Peruvian Armed Forces — the first time Bangladesh had exported a domestically built defense robot to another nation's military.
